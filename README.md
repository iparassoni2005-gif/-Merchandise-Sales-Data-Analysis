## 📌 Project Overview
 
This project performs a **comprehensive data analysis on Merchandise Sales data** using Python. It covers end-to-end analysis including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer insights, product performance, statistical hypothesis testing, and profitability analysis.
 
---
 
## 🎯 Objectives
 
- Perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on merchandise sales dataset
- Analyze **sales trends** across product categories and time periods
- Derive **customer insights** based on age, gender, and location
- Identify **top 10 best-selling products** by revenue
- Conduct **statistical hypothesis testing** (T-Test, One-Way ANOVA, Two-Way ANOVA)
- Analyze **profit distribution** and **product ratings**

## 📊 Analysis Steps
 
### Step 1 — Data Loading & Preprocessing
- Loaded `merch_sales.csv` dataset using Pandas
- Converted `Order Date` to datetime format
- Performed data overview: shape, info, describe, null value check
### Step 2 — Sales Analysis
- **Total Sales by Product Category** — Bar chart visualization
- **Sales Trend Over Time** — Monthly aggregated line chart
### Step 3 — Customer Insights
- **Age vs Spending** — Analyzed buyer age impact on total sales
- **Gender vs Sales** — Compared sales distribution by gender
- **Top 10 Locations** — Identified highest revenue-generating locations
### Step 4 — Statistical Analysis
- **One-Sample T-Test** — Tested product ratings against population mean
- **One-Way ANOVA** — Compared sales across North, South, East regions
- **Two-Way ANOVA** — Analyzed interaction between Region & Product on Sales
### Step 5 — Product Insights
- **Top 10 Products** — Best performing products by total sales
- **Profit Distribution** — Histogram with KDE for profit analysis
### Step 6 — Ratings & Reviews
- **Average Rating by Category** — Category-wise rating comparison
- **Rating Distribution** — Count plot of all product ratings
---
 
## 💡 Key Insights Derived
 
- Identified **top 3 product categories** driving maximum revenue
- Found **peak sales months** for better inventory planning
- Statistical tests revealed **significant regional sales differences** (ANOVA)
- Top 10 products contribute to **majority of total revenue (Pareto Principle)**
- Customer age group analysis highlighted **high-value buyer segments**
